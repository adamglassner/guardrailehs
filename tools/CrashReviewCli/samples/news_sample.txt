Three-vehicle crash closes part of Route 9 (SAMPLE - TEST DATA)
A three-vehicle crash shut down the southbound lanes of Route 9 near Exit 14 for two hours on Tuesday evening, state police said.
According to police, a blue Honda Civic was traveling too fast for conditions when it rear-ended a silver minivan that had slowed for traffic. The impact pushed the minivan into a black pickup truck ahead of it.
The driver of the Civic, a 24-year-old man, was charged with reckless driving and following too closely. Police said speed was a factor.
The minivan driver and a passenger suffered minor injuries. The pickup driver was not hurt.
